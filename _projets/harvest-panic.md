  A short game I'm making for my portfolio, from scratch in Blueprints with Fab's assets.
  a small farm and its wheat fields. You harvest the fields yourself while crows come in
  waves to eat the wheat. Harvested wheat earns you coins, and any wheat the crows eat
  has to be paid back with interest.

contributions:
  - Crow AI built with a Behavior Tree
  - Game manager in the Game Instance (coins, what you owe, unlocked fields)
  - Harvesting tools you can upgrade to widen their range
  - "Throwable vegetables bought in a shop: simple shots first, then explosive or multi-shot throws, up to the watermelon"
  - Fields rated from 1 to 10 in difficulty, unlocked one after another
